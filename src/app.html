How a ride works

  1. You hear a word and get a few seconds to recall it.
  2. You hear the definition, an example and a memory hook.
  3. Rate it 1 (nailed it) to 5 (blank), out loud or with a tap.

Weak words come back every day until you've rated them strong on two different days. New words are held back while your weak list is long.
